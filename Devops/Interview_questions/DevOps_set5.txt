most asked interview questions for 2+ years of experience in CI/CD, Linux, Docker, and Jenkins:

CI/CD:

1 What is the difference between continuous integration, continuous delivery, and continuous deployment?
2 How do you manage and automate CI/CD pipelines across multiple environments?
3 How do you implement rollbacks in a CI/CD pipeline?
4 What are some common CI/CD tools, and why would you choose one over the others?
5 How do you monitor and debug issues in a CI/CD pipeline?

Linux: 

1How do you check the available disk space and memory usage in Linux?
2What is the difference between grep, find, and awk commands?
3How do you set up and manage a cron job?
4Explain the difference between chmod, chown, and chgrp.
5How do you analyze and resolve high CPU or memory usage in Linux?

Docker:

1What is the difference between Docker images and containers?
2How do you create and optimize a Dockerfile for a specific application?
3What is the purpose of Docker Compose, and how is it used?
4How do you handle container networking in Docker?
5Explain the difference between bind mounts, volumes, and tmpfs in Docker.

Jenkins:

1How do you set up a Jenkins pipeline to deploy an application?
2What is the difference between a freestyle job and a pipeline in Jenkins?
3How do you secure a Jenkins server?
4How do you configure Jenkins agents, and what are their purposes?
5What is Blue Ocean in Jenkins, and how is it used?
